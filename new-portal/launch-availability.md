# Launch Availability

**Last reviewed: July 27, 2026**

The new GearHost control panel is in launch preview. This page distinguishes
what customers can see in the current preview from the items still receiving
final launch testing.

Availability can vary by account, plan, region, and service configuration.
If a control is not shown for your account, contact GearHost Support before
depending on it for production.

## Available in the current preview

- Account registration, email sign-in, password recovery, and the sign-in
  providers shown on the login page
- Dashboard and service navigation
- CloudSite creation with Web Hosting, Email, and DNS service choices
- Web, file, DNS, email, account, and activity management surfaces
- Security options shown in account settings, including multifactor and
  passkey controls where enabled

These items are visible in the current preview. Individual service
configurations may still require support assistance.

## Receiving final launch validation

The following areas exist in the new control panel or are part of the launch
plan, but should not yet be treated as universally available:

| Area | Launch-preview status |
|------|-----------------------|
| Application deployment | Runtime templates and end-to-end deployments are being tested across supported application types. |
| Custom domains and SSL | Automated SSL is receiving final testing across new, custom, and migrated domain configurations. |
| Databases | SQL Server and MySQL creation, credentials, and management are being validated across the new server environment. |
| Billing | Service and invoice views are present. Payment setup and the handling of existing balances and credits require final confirmation. |
| High availability | Load balancing and multi-zone behavior require final launch confirmation before production use. |
| Support | Support, ticket, and chat surfaces are being prepared for launch. Availability may vary during the preview. |
| Existing-service migration | Existing services remain in the current control panel until GearHost schedules and confirms their migration. |

## Planned beyond the initial launch

- Advanced support that automatically diagnoses and repairs hosting problems
- A fully automated migration path for every legacy service configuration
- Expanded documentation inside the new support experience

## What this means for existing customers

You do not need to move an existing service or recreate it in the new control
panel today. GearHost will use a phased migration and will contact customers
before their services move.

The existing control panel remains the source of truth for services that have
not been migrated. New services created in the launch preview are separate
unless GearHost Support confirms that they are part of a migration.

Questions about a specific service? Email
[support@gearhost.com](mailto:support@gearhost.com).
